Definitions: mid-neck = cross-section 3 cm under the chin line; waist = smallest-circumference section between
10 cm and 0.5 cm under the chin line; trapezius shelf = how far the top of the trap at the neck wall (|x| 11 cm) sits
below the chin line (larger = more neck exposed); visible neck length = chin line down to where the silhouette reaches
85 % of the head half width (front) / the nape moves 4 cm behind its chin-line position (side).
Hump: protrusion = how far the central back (|x| < 2.5 cm) sits behind a straight chord from the lumbar curve (most forward
back point) to the nape at the chin line, maximum over the upper back; back-most z = most posterior point of the central back.
"After" rows include BOTH fields (neck + hump). Note: "before" mid-neck widths are huge because the trapezius flares
straight into the jaw line (no neck column).
Realistic athletic reference: neck width 0.75-0.85 of head width, circumference 40-46 cm (head 57-60 cm).
